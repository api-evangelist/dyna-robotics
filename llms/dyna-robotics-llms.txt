# Dyna Robotics

> Dyna Robotics is a Redwood City, California robotics AI company founded in 2024 by Lindon Gao, York Yang and Jason Ma. It builds robot foundation models — DYNA-1, a vision-language-action (VLA) model — that drive pairs of stationary robotic arms through high-dexterity commercial tasks in laundry, food service and factory environments. The company sells robotics-as-a-service and, as of this profile, publishes no public developer API.
